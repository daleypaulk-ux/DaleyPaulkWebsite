Process Consistency

Automation: Repeatable software-driven rules and checks.
Manual: Greater operator variability over time.

Operational Workload

Automation: Less repetitive manual handling after setup.
Manual: Ongoing high-touch process demand.

Control Surface

Automation: Configurable controls and monitoring points.
Manual: Direct discretionary control with higher cognitive load.

Best Fit

Automation: Teams prioritizing repeatable execution process.
